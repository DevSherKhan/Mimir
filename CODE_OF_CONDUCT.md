# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Mimir contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, welcome constructive feedback, assume good intent, and protect other people's privacy. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces, as well as when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or lock contributions and conversations that violate this standard. Repeated or severe violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's moderation tools. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with appropriate confidentiality.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by established open-source community codes of conduct and GitHub's Community Guidelines.
